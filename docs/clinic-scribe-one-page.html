One workflow, connected across devices

Designed for supervised clinic pilots

01

Encounter-bound mobile capture

Recording stays locked until patient, active visit, and consent are confirmed. If an interruption occurs, the clinician can retry or discard retained audio.

02

Transcription and draft documentation

Configurable ElevenLabs Scribe v2 Medical support, multilingual transcription, detected-language details, clinical entities, and SOAP-style draft notes.

03

Web encounter review

Review visits, linked transcription jobs, analysis, and note status from a clinic workspace built for draft-to-final review.

04

Recovery and human oversight

Retry safeguards help keep a capture attached to its confirmed visit. Coding and decision-support outputs are advisory—not a substitute for clinical judgment.

EHR and provider setup are organization-specific

OCHIN/Epic SMART on FHIR access requires an approved app registration and clinic configuration. Clinical-audio processing requires the organization to verify provider agreements and retention settings before any protected health information is used.

Pilot status and important limits: An Android preview checklist was reported complete on a Pixel 9a; iOS device validation remains pending. This overview is not a claim of HIPAA or other regulatory certification. Production use requires organization review of applicable agreements, privacy controls, EHR authorization, and device validation. AI-generated content requires clinician review.
